Location and sunshine

Get daily irradiation on the tilted plane for your site from PVGIS (Monthly radiation, your tilt angle) and enter it below. The sizing uses the lowest month, so the system works all year.

A tilt close to your latitude is a good all-year starting point.

Efficiencies

Typical values are pre-filled. Replace them with datasheet values if you have them.

Extra losses are not in the original method. Real systems lose 10–20% to heat and dust, so 10 is a sensible value when you want a more cautious design. 0 matches the report.

System design

Choose the DC voltage and the building blocks. Higher voltage means lower current and cheaper cables.

The conservative method treats cable and inverter losses as extra energy the battery must supply, so it gives a larger bank. A margin of 25% is common for inverters.
